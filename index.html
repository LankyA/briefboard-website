How it works

Built for the way patrol actually operates.

Tiered Notifications
Four priority tiers — from immediate officer safety alerts to routine patrol guidance — each with distinct notification behavior so officers always know what demands attention right now.
Structured Intelligence Feed
ATIs, ATLs, RFIs, CIAC bulletins, stolen vehicles, directed patrol — each content type in its own lane. No digging through email chains. No missed critical updates buried in inbox clutter.
Warrant Database
Searchable, CBI-synced warrant records on every officer's iPhone. Name and DOB search with full warrant detail — available on a traffic stop without a radio call.
Supervisor Web Console
Supervisors post intelligence from any browser. District targeting, expiration controls, acknowledgment tracking. The same briefing that used to take an email list and a prayer — done in under two minutes.
